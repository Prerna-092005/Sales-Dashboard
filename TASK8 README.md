    "# Task 8 – Sales Dashboard (Power BI)\n",
    "\n",
    "## Overview:\n",
    "This project is part of the Data Analyst Internship tasks. The objective was to build a clean, interactive sales dashboard that visually presents performance by product, region, and month using Power BI.\n",
    "\n",
    "## Tools Used:\n",
    "- Power BI Desktop\n",
    "- Jupyter Notebook (for generating insights)\n",
    "\n",
    "## Dataset:\n",
    "- Superstore Sales Dataset (with columns like Order Date, Region, Product, Segment, Sales, Profit, Quantity)\n",
    "\n",
    "## Dashboard Features:\n",
    "- **KPIs**: Total Sales ($12.64M), Profit ($1.47M), and Quantity Sold (178K)\n",
    "- **Visuals**:\n",
    "  - Bar Chart: Top 5 Customers\n",
    "  - Donut Charts: Sales by Product, Profit by Segment\n",
    "  - Map Visual: Sales by Region (Global View)\n",
    "  - Column Chart: Bottom 5 Products\n",
    "  - Line Graph: Profit Trend by Region\n",
    "- **Slicer**: Interactive filter by Region\n",
    "- **Design**: Clean theme, consistent color usage, and logical visual hierarchy\n",
    "\n",
    "## Key Insights:\n",
    "1. The Global Superstore achieved total sales of $12.64M and profits of $1.47M, with 178K units sold.\n",
    "2. 'Cubify Cube' was the top-selling product, contributing 37.93% of the Top 5 sales.\n",
    "3. The 'Home Office' segment generated the highest profit, while the 'Corporate' segment was the least profitable.\n",
    "4. Most of the sales were concentrated in North America and Western Europe, with limited activity in Eastern Africa.\n",
    "\n",
    "## Files Included:\n",
